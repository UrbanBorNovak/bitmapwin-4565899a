# Vision: The Multiverse on Bitcoin

BITMAPWIN is **not** an RPG with a token attached, and it is not "play-to-earn". Mixing the two usually gives you the metaverse no-man's land: gamers find it a chore and Bitcoiners don't care about the yield.

What we build instead is **third places**: spaces outside home and work where people come back to meet, talk, show what they made, and do business.

## Principles

1. **Public commons and private rooms.** The Oasis, the Grandstand and the Bit-Café are open to everyone. Private rooms open only with a Bitcoin proof: a BIP-322 signature, a held BITMAP, or a device-held Shadow pass. No admin decides who gets in.
2. **Every BITMAP is a custom build.** A BITMAP can be a gallery, a radio lounge, a garden, or an office. Builds are architecture, not loot.
3. **You can just hang out.** No daily quests or battle passes, and nothing pushes you to grind. Sitting in your seat and watching the stream is enough.
4. **Real commerce settles on Bitcoin.** Trades happen through PSBTs, inscriptions and sats paid to the treasury. BIT is an off-chain community credit and never stands in for money.
5. **Only chain facts.** No mock data, and the site never trusts an ownership claim made by the browser.

## Non-goals

- Fake in-browser mining or CPU hash loops
- Speculative in-game tokens
- Real-time CAD or physics in shared scenes (we keep shared scenes at 60 FPS)
