# Richer Voxel Creature Models

## Goal
Make the procedural voxel wildlife and Block-DNA Guardians visibly richer at close range without changing their movement, fly-brain behavior, scale, or gameplay.

## Changes
- Add more articulated voxel anatomy: segmented limbs, paws/hooves, joints, muzzles, ears, tails, wings, claws, horns, plates, and facial features.
- Give each wildlife species stronger identifying details, including elephant ears/trunk/tusks, rhino armor and horns, lion mane and paws, giraffe ossicones and markings, rabbit legs and ears, vulture feathers, and scorpion pincers/stinger.
- Refine all Guardian families with layered armor, joints, runes, crests, wing feathers, segmented tails, claws, and rarity-aware accents.
- Keep existing colors, block-derived Guardian DNA, fly-brain movement, collision behavior, and immutable on-chain models unchanged.
- Reuse geometry and keep added detail within the mobile WebGL budget.

## Verification
- Check representative wildlife and Guardian models in the live 3D viewers on desktop and phone.
- Confirm the preview builds cleanly and no new runtime errors appear.
